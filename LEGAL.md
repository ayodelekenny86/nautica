## 2. Refund Policy

### Eligible for refund
You may request a refund if:
- The order was cancelled by the seller or driver before delivery, or
- Payment was taken but no delivery was attempted, or
- The delivered quantity was materially short of the ordered amount.

### Not eligible for refund
Refunds are not available for:
- Orders already delivered in full, or
- Cancellations requested after a driver has been dispatched, or
- Change of mind after payment confirmation.

### How to request
Contact support at 0545009046 or use Help & support in the app. Refunds are reviewed within one business day and credited to the original payment method when approved.
